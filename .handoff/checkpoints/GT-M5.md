# GT card — M5 Railway deploy (try it)
Link: https://app-production-7e47.up.railway.app (deployed b67ab03; /api/version proves it)

1. Open the link in Chrome. A sign-in box appears: type anything as the username and your access code as the password.
2. Allow the microphone, tick "I'm wearing headphones", keep Voice-to-voice, press Start. Say "What day works for your appointment?" → Spanish audio; the orb turns iridescent; the sealed card shows first audio and end of audio only.
3. Say "Necesito cambiar mi cita para el jueves." → English audio.
4. Switch to Cascade mid-session. Say "Can you come in Thursday at three?" → the three stage cards light up in turn with ms; the terminal logs each stage.
5. Error case: open the link in a private window and press Cancel at the sign-in box → "access code required"; nothing loads.
Reply "works", or the step number and what you saw.
